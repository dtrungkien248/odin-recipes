LASAGNA



This slow cooker lasagna has layers of tender noodles, rich tomato sauce, and melted cheese. Best of all, the slow cooker does the cooking.


Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef

  • 1 medium onion, chopped

  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic

  • 1 (29 ounce) can tomato sauce

  • 1 (6 ounce) can tomato paste

  • 1 ½ teaspoons salt

  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano

  • 16 ounces shredded mozzarella cheese

  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

  • 1 pound lean ground beef

  • 1 (12 ounce) package lasagna noodles


  1. Step 1: Gather all ingredients
  2. Step 2: Cook and stir ground beef, onion, and garlic in a large skillet over medium heat until meat is crumbly and browned.
  3. Step 3: Add tomato sauce, tomato paste, salt, and oregano; stir until well combined and warmed through. Remove from heat.
  4. Step 4: Stir mozzarella, cottage cheese, and Parmesan together in a large bowl until well combined. Set aside.
  5. Step 5: Spread a layer of meat sauce onto the bottom of a slow cooker.
  6. Step 6: Add a double layer of uncooked lasagna noodles, breaking noodles to fit into the cooker as needed.
  7. Step 7: Top noodles with a portion of cheese mixture.
  8. Step 8: Repeat the layering of sauce, noodles, and cheese until all of the ingredients are used.
  9. Step 9: Cover and cook on Low until noodles are tender, 4 to 6 hours.
